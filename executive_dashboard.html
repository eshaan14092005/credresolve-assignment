What we are confident about

Seven of nine operating metrics are flat. One — PTP rate — is down 6.6%. Two cannot be measured at all.

No variable in the data predicts recovery. Correlations with collections activity run between −0.002 and +0.009. Agent performance varies 0.98× what chance alone would produce.

Recommendation on the ₹10 Cr

Do not deploy it into any of the six options yet. No cost field exists anywhere in the source systems, so ROI and break-even cannot be computed for any of them.

Commit ₹50–75 lakh — under 8% — to a six-arm randomised test over 12 weeks, then deploy the remainder against the result.
